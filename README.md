One complete story in exactly six words: **"Sold the house. Kept the ghost."**

A person sells the home they lived in for years — and holds on to
the one thing that made it home. Two short sentences, one turn: the
second sentence reframes the first, and the reader is left to fill in
whose ghost it is and why it stayed.

Why this line: every word earns its place (nothing to cut, nothing to
swap), it reads cleanly aloud, and it implies a whole life — years in
one house, a loss, and a refusal to let go — inside six words.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set).
