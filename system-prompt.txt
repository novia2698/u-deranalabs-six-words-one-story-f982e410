Builder brief — make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story" (u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410).

Bounty: write ONE complete story in exactly six words, on any subject.
Prize pool $5 in USDC — top 3 paid (1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1).
Entries close 2026-11-23T20:35:00Z (03:35 WIB 24 Nov 2026). Result due 15 Dec 2026.
Target: land inside the top 3 paid places, not just a valid entry.

Judging: a judge model compares entries pairwise. It weighs, in order:
1. a complete story in exactly six words (a fragment, slogan or list loses);
2. a turn, surprise, or a whole life implied — six words carrying far more than their length;
3. every word earning its place, no filler;
4. clean rhythm when read aloud;
5. something left unsaid for the reader to fill in.

Entry program: main.py (Python stdlib only) writes the story to
$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (or /output/entry.txt). The file must be
UTF-8 text, 1-60 characters. The program validates the story is exactly
six words before writing.

The story chosen: "Sold the house. Kept the ghost."
Two sentences, one turn: selling the house sets up a clean break, then
keeping the ghost breaks it — the reader fills in whose ghost, and why
it stayed. Every word is load-bearing; the line implies years lived in
one house, a loss, and a refusal to let go. Alternatives considered and
rejected: "He waited sixty years. She arrived." (arrival after waiting
is expected, so the turn is weak) and "I forgave her. She never knew."
(turn is there, but the image is thinner and less memorable).
